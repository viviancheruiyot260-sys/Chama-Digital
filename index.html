How It Works

From registration to verified monthly savings in four steps

Simple enough for first-time smartphone users, structured enough for group officials.

01

Choose Registration Path

Register as a Chama Official (Chairperson, Treasurer, or Secretary) or apply as a Member with your National ID and invite code.

02

Chairperson Approval

New member accounts enter Pending Approval until the Chairperson reviews and activates their membership.

03

Contribute & Track Loans

Pick a contribution month, pay via M-Pesa STK Push or USSD, update your savings balance, and manage group loans.

04

Receipts & Offline USSD

Download or print official receipts anytime, or dial *384*240# when mobile data is unavailable.

Inclusive Financial Access

Access Chama Digital Without Internet

Not every chama member always has mobile data or a smartphone. Members can dial our USSD shortcode *384*240# from any phone to:

  • 01. Check personal savings balance and monthly target
  • 02. Check outstanding loan balance and next repayment date
  • 03. View contribution status and make M-Pesa contributions
  • 04. Review recent payment history and repayment dates
