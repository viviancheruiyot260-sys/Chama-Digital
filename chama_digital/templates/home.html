About Us

Built around community trust, transparent books, and shared prosperity

For generations, Kenyan chamas and table-banking groups have helped families build businesses, pay school fees, and invest together. Chama Digital preserves that community spirit while removing lost exercise books, unverified receipts, and manual arithmetic errors.

How It Works

From registration to verified monthly savings in four steps

Simple enough for first-time smartphone users, structured enough for group officials.

01

Choose Registration Path

Register as a Chama Official (with authorization code) or apply as a Member with your National ID and group invite code.

02

Chairperson Approval

New member accounts enter Pending Approval until the Chairperson reviews and activates their membership number.

03

Contribute & Track Loans

Pay via M-Pesa or submit Bank Transfer proof for Treasurer verification, track savings, and manage group loans.

04

Receipts & Offline USSD

Download official PDF receipts anytime, or check the USSD gateway preview ({{ ussd_shortcode }}) for low-connectivity phones.
